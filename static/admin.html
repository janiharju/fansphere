💡 How Club Realms Work

  • When a supporter signs in or edits their profile, they pick their Club Realm.
  • Selecting a club realm isolates their forum feed and live chat reactions to that specific club's supporters.
  • Users can also select "All Realms (View All Clubs)" in their profile to see cross-club discussion.
  • Any changes made here sync across all active browsers and persist permanently to disk.
